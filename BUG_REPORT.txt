Date tested: 2026-10-28
Scope: Root static website (index.html, script.js, styles.css)

Bug 1: Sport & wellness facility image returns 404
---------------------------------------------------
Severity: Medium
Status: Reproduced

Location:
- index.html, facility card for "Sport & wellness"
- Image URL:
  https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1526676037777-05a232554f77?auto=format&fit=crop&w=700&q=80

Symptoms:
- The Sport & wellness card appears in the Life on campus section.
- Its image request returns HTTP 404.
- Users see a broken image instead of the facility photo.
- Opening the card's lightbox also uses the same unavailable Unsplash photo ID, so the enlarged image cannot load either.

Steps to reproduce:
1. Open index.html in a browser.
2. Scroll to "Life on campus".
3. Inspect the Sport & wellness card or its network requests.
4. Observe that the image request responds with 404.

Expected result:
- The card and lightbox display a valid sports/wellness image.

Recommended fix:
- Replace both data-image and img src values for this card with a verified image URL.
- Confirm the replacement returns HTTP 200 before release.

Evidence:
- Browser request capture on 2026-10-28:
  HTTP 404 for the w=700 image URL above.

Bug 2: Admissions content is outdated for the current site date
----------------------------------------------------------------
Severity: Low
Status: Reproduced by content inspection

Location:
- index.html, admissions banner and Important dates section

Symptoms:
- The page advertises "Admissions 2025–26".
- The important dates are 15 Jan 2025, 30 Apr 2025, and 15 Jun 2025.
- The page footer also says "© 2025 ABC College".
- The current test date is 2026-10-28, so visitors may be given expired application information.

Expected result:
- Admissions year, deadlines, start date, and footer copyright are updated for the active academic cycle, or the page clearly identifies the site as an archived/demo page.

Recommended fix:
- Replace the sample dates and academic year with verified current dates.
- Update the footer year.
- If this remains a demonstration site, make the demo status prominent near the admissions call to action rather than relying only on smaller sample-content notes.
